Research Interests

  • Development of **Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs)** for **modeling** and **simulation** of **dielectric materials**.
  • **Deconvolution** under **constraints** to reconstruct **charge distributions** from **electroacoustic (PEA) measurements**.
  • **Machine learning** and **AI techniques** for **parameter identification** and **model optimization**.
  • **Global sensitivity analysis** to determine the most **influential model parameters**.
